-------------------------------About this App-------------------------------
Web-based planner for organizing tasks, schedules, and personal productivity.
This is a personal productivity and reflection platform that helps users track time, activities, mood, and progress over time.

And NO, this is not another to-do app list. It's a life analytic tool. Which helps people(like me) to undrestand where did
my time went.

It points to your personal productivity, and again NO, this is not about scheduling, this is more about
what you did in the past, and judges you give you realistic feedback. (Yes its harsh. Drop all the sugercoat if you wanna be someone who stands out)

A later feature:
Example:>
"Based on previous off-days after a work shift, there is a 70% chance you will spend the afternoon sleeping."
or
"When you work on Laravel for at least 15 minutes before opening social media, your average productive time doubles."
Notice that these predictions are less "You will do X tomorrow" and more:
"Here's what usually happens."
That feels more useful and less creepy.
